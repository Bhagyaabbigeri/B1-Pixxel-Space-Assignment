## Decision record
- **Approach chosen and why:** Combined Python scripting (`tm_limits.yaml` limits, 3-sample persistence, derivative/flatline checks) with manual CSV verification. Limit checks catch the payload overheat (`SE-2`) and OBC reset (`SE-3`), but miss the stuck `0.9A` heater (`SE-1`, which stays under `1.2A` until SoC drops below `70%` 26h later) and the 3h `18.40°C` flatline (`DQ-3`).
- **Alternatives considered and why rejected:** Rejected relying solely on raw `tm_limits.yaml` crossings because it flags 1-sample glitches (`26.88V`, Row 15246) while missing the stuck heater. Rejected interpolating across the 1,510s gap because linear interpolation across 25m distorts orbital slopes.
- **What breaks at scale (10x satellites, 10x data, a new operator):** At 60 satellites or 1Hz cadence, manual CSV checks fail and new operators drown in 1-sample alarms. Scaling requires automated pipelines that sort/deduplicate packets on ingest, enforce 3-sample persistence, run sliding-window flatline (`std==0`) and SoC trend checks, and push ranked alerts to a dashboard.
